# Law-aware evidence presentations

[Contract algebra](EVIDENCE-ALGEBRA.md) · [Transport](EVIDENCE-TRANSPORT.md)

## Problem, scope and established foundations

An interface may expose several flags derived from the same facts. Treating the
flags as independent admits impossible public states and can break conditional
composition. Infer the exact image of the declared summary map, reason modulo
its laws, and distinguish impossible public states from genuinely hidden future
constraints. A schema-valid flag vector is still not authenticated current data.

This adds an explicit `.present(bindings)` factory on an evidence session. It
returns a law-aware contract algebra, not a reinterpretation of existing handles,
a new Hindley–Milner type system, or an automatic optimizer for arbitrary source.
Finite distributive lattices, Heyting algebras, p-morphisms and decision diagrams
are established theory [1–3]. Historical priority for this integration is not
claimed. The following proofs are mathematical proofs, not proof-assistant output.

## Exact schema and a quotient of positive contracts

Let X=P(D) be the private Boolean poset, Y=P(E) the public Boolean poset, and
phi:X->Y the monotone map whose coordinates are the declared summary formulas.
Let R=phi(X), with the order inherited from Y. The exact schema I(T) holds iff
T is in R. I need not be monotone: duplicated summaries give the law x=y, which
accepts {} and {x,y} but rejects each singleton.

Two positive public contracts are equivalent precisely when they agree on R,
or equivalently when their substitutions along phi agree on every private state.
Restriction rho:Up(Y)->Up(R) preserves finite meets, joins and constants and is
surjective. Indeed any upset A of R is the restriction of its upward closure
in Y. Thus Up(R) is the bounded-distributive quotient by this equivalence.
Its elements are not generally a Heyting quotient using the *old* implication.
For duplicated x:=z,y:=z, x and y become equal, yet old residual(x,y)=y whereas
old residual(x,x)=true. Residuals must be recomputed with the new semantics.

For an upward public formula A, define its canonical representative

    C(A) = upward_Y(R intersection A).

Then C(A) agrees with A on R, C(C(A))=C(A), and C(A)=C(B) iff A and B agree on R.
The proof uses upward closure in both directions. In particular, if S is in R
and above a witness T in R intersection A, monotonicity gives A(S).
Canonical representatives also reflect order. Interning their reduced ordered
BDDs therefore makes handle identity decide equality within one presentation.
Conjunction and disjunction are followed by C. Always means C(true), not
necessarily the ambient constant true at impossible valuations.

## The correct conditional operation

For A,B in Up(R), define

    (A =>_R B)(S) iff every T in R above S satisfies A(T) implies B(T).

For every W in Up(R), W intersection A subset B iff W subset (A =>_R B).
Proof: upward closure carries W from S to T for the forward implication; taking
T=S proves the reverse. This is the Heyting adjunction on the finite image poset.
Its implementation uses a Boolean interior followed by canonicalization:

    C(interior_Y(not I or not A or B)).

Here interior_Y(F)(S)=forall T containing S, F(T). On a decision node with low
L and high H, its low branch is interior(L) AND interior(H), and its high branch
is interior(H). The dual upward closure has branches upward(L) and
upward(L) OR upward(H). Splitting on whether the variable is in S proves both
recurrences. Schema constraints may be nonmonotone internally; public contracts
remain monotone on R and have monotone canonical representatives on Y.

Example: if x and y both mean private z, their canonical handles coincide and
residual(x,y) is the presentation's always. A generated public predicate MUST
still reject impossible input x=true,y=false; simplification is conditional on
schema validity. A private predicate generated by composing with phi needs no
schema check, since phi(S) is in R by construction.

## Static laws versus future behavior

Substitution from Up(R) to Up(X) always preserves positive operations. It preserves
all residuals exactly iff

    for every S in X and T in R with phi(S) subset T,
    some U containing S satisfies phi(U)=T.                    (L)

This is the established p-morphism condition for the *image* codomain [1].
Sufficiency follows by lifting any quantified public extension before applying
the private residual. For necessity, choose an unliftable T above phi(S), put
A=up_R(T), B=up_R(T) minus {T}, and compare residuals at S. Every private extension
satisfying A has view strictly above T, so satisfies B. The public point T itself
violates A=>_R B. These give executable separating positive formulas.

**Static-schema repair criterion.** Some sound static restriction Z of Y,
containing every realizable view and using inherited inclusion order, makes
transport preserve residuals iff (L) holds. Necessity: every target in R is
still in Z, so lifting through Z implies (L). Sufficiency: choose Z=R.
Thus the exact-image test decides whether ANY such state-only law schema can
repair the interface without removing private states. It is stronger than
checking that all public flags are globally realizable.

Duplicated flags fail the old independent-cube audit but pass (L): the impossible
singletons disappear. In contrast, x:=a,y:=a AND b still fails on its exact image.
From S={b}, the view {} cannot extend to the *globally realizable* view {x},
witnessed elsewhere by private {a}. Adding a would also enable y. No sound
state-only law can remove this target, so laws alone cannot repair that failure.

Adding a new summary z:=b CAN now repair the latter map on its newly inferred
image: it reveals enough private information for order reflection. More generally,
an order-reflecting map has (L) onto its image, since phi(S)<=phi(V) implies S<=V;
choose U=V. This does not contradict the old non-repair theorem for the full
independent public cube: the codomain semantics has deliberately changed.

Do not check only one-bit extensions of R: a cover in the image can require
several flags at once. The duplicated-flags diagonal is the simplest example.
The audit quantifies actual image targets rather than reusing the old one-bit test.

## Symbolic image and transport algorithms

Copy the authenticated private summary DAGs into an isolated bounded Boolean
arena. Construct an exact public image BDD by recursively fixing public flags:
false adds not phi_e to the private condition; true adds phi_e. At a leaf accept
iff that condition is satisfiable. Before recursing, existentially forget private
variables absent from all remaining summaries. Eliminating them commutes with
future conjunctions that do not mention them. Memoizing position and remaining
condition avoids enumerating irrelevant assignment histories. Induction proves
the resulting public BDD equals I, not merely a necessary set of pairwise laws.

For (L), symbolic current S, image witness V, and future U use interleaved copies
of private variables. Search for

    phi(S) <= phi(V) AND NOT exists U: S<=U AND phi(U)=phi(V).

A satisfying assignment supplies both the blocked current state and a private
witness that its requested view is globally realizable. Recheck the exact fiber
above that concrete current state before returning the obstruction. A positive
result is a computed audit, not a portable independently checkable proof object.

The support-overlap components of the summaries factor this problem. Different
components depend on disjoint private variables, so their images are products
and lifts combine by union of private extensions. Conversely a failed component
extends to a global failure by fixing other private variables false. Constants
contribute singleton factors. A lone nonconstant monotone summary always lifts
onto its image: enable its whole support if a true view is requested. These
proved shortcuts are reported separately from relational checks.

## API, representation and trust

`privateAlgebra.present([{atom,value}, ...])` returns an independent presentation.
Names and private-owned handles follow the existing summary validation rules.
No new nodes are added to the caller's private algebra, even after failure.

The presentation provides `atom`, `always`, `never`, `all`, `any`, `fromFrontier`,
`equivalent`, `entails`, `counterexample`, `residual`, `minimum`, `evaluate`, `view`,
`isRealizable`, `inspect`, `inspectLaws`, `auditTransport`, `source`, `sourcePrivate`,
and `stats`. Handles belong to exactly one presentation; ordinary algebra handles,
foreign presentation handles, clones and raw node indices are rejected.

`evaluate(contract,truePublicAtoms)` rejects unrealizable flag vectors with a
TypeError instead of interpreting a quotient representative outside its domain.
`isRealizable` tests the schema; `view(truePrivateAtoms)` computes the current
summary flags in the Boolean model. Counterexamples and minimum-price results
are realizable public vectors. `minimum` prices *true public flags*, defaults to
one, and breaks ties by fewer flags then low branches; it is not an acquisition
plan for private evidence. `inspectLaws` is an immutable BDD snapshot, not an
exponential enumeration or an import/trust format.

`source(name,contract)` generates an ordinary Boolean function on public facts
with `require schema predicate`. The schema check cannot be erased merely because
the quotient predicate is always. `sourcePrivate` composes with the declared
meanings and generates a predicate over private fact fields. For residuals, that
is the pullback of the IMAGE residual, not automatically the private residual;
use the audit for the all-formula commutation guarantee. Runtime flags are not
authentication, verified refinement types or proof that external data was checked.

A generic BDD emitter uses lazy ordered Boolean decisions, including internal
negative schema tests. It factors immediate branches that share a continuation
when that preserves the same decision order. This new emitter does not change
existing source generators. Quotient canonicalization can reorder/demand different
partial facts from handwritten code; truth semantics assumes pure total facts.
Explicit `require` is still needed to guard a returned value. Host effects remain
explicit, all demanded loops share the normal invocation allowance, and no guest
graph, allocator, closure ABI or prime arithmetic is introduced.

## Resources and compatibility

At most 128 private and public atoms; the receiving private session's existing
maxNodes and maxWork configure the presentation. Two retained arenas each have
that node cap (default 8192, maximum 65536 including terminals). Operations use
one shared work counter (default 200000, maximum 2000000) across Boolean work and
traversals. Each ephemeral audit arena has the same separate cap; components are
processed sequentially. Recursion is bounded by vocabularies (at most 384 levels
in a triple-state relation). These are structural work limits, not wall-clock limits.

On failure, additions to both retained arenas are rolled back and operation-local
memo tables are discarded. Earlier handles remain usable. Successful intermediate
nodes count toward session capacity; retiring the presentation releases its state.
Contract/frontier lists are bounded to 4096, source generation to 512 reachable
nodes across the demanded predicate and schema. Existing compiler, ABI and source
limits still apply independently. Intermediate diagrams may be exponential;
limits throw rather than return a weaker schema or a false proof of portability.

No parser, inference, JTE, existing emitter, ABI, effects, default lowering,
dependency, workflow or root README change. Alternative designs include assuming
independent flags (the earlier API), trusting hand-written laws (can exclude real
states unsoundly), enumerating all views (useful only as a small test oracle), or
tracking histories and transitions (needed when static laws cannot repair loss).

## Verification plan

Enumerate small monotone summary maps and compare exact images with explicit
private truth tables. Compare canonical equality, residual adjunctions and prices
against all upsets of the image. Independently enumerate relative lifts and
replay negative transport witnesses at formula level. Cover duplicate/constant
summaries, hidden future blockers, more-informative repair and multi-bit image
covers. Execute generated public and private predicates, including schema-invalid
flags even for always, through actual Wasm. Exercise all eight lowering modes,
budgets, demand, projection, diagnostics, effects, ABI, namespaces, rollback and
large structured images. Run complete Node, host/reducer and available browser
suites. Record actual results separately; do not infer worldwide originality.
